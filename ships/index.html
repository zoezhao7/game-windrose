Windrose Ships Guide: Sloop, Brigantine & Frigate (2026)

Your ship is your home, your weapon, and your lifeline in Windrose. Choosing the right vessel and configuring it properly can mean the difference between dominating the seas and sinking to the depths. This guide covers all three ship classes, their stats, roles, and optimal configurations.

Naval Combat Overview

Naval combat in Windrose is a two-layered system that blends ranged cannon warfare with close-quarters boarding combat. Understanding how these layers interact is essential for victory.

Ranged Cannon Combat

All ships are equipped with cannons that fire broadside. Positioning is critical — you want your side facing the enemy while minimizing your own exposure. Different cannon types (standard shot, chain shot for sails, heavy shot for armor) give you tactical options depending on your goal: disabling, boarding, or destroying.

Boarding & Melee Combat

Once an enemy ship's armor is sufficiently depleted, you can grapple and board for melee combat. Boarding allows you to capture ships intact, loot more resources, and avoid sinking valuable targets. Your crew's melee stats and equipment determine boarding success.

Seamless Land-Ship Switching

Windrose features seamless transitions between land exploration and naval gameplay. You can dock at any coastline, disembark to explore islands on foot, and return to your ship without loading screens. This enables fluid gameplay where naval positioning sets up land encounters and vice versa.

Crew Management

Each ship has a crew capacity that determines how many crew members you can bring. Larger crews allow specialization — dedicated gunners, repair crews, and boarding parties. Smaller crews on ships like the Sloop require multi-role crew members who can switch between tasks quickly.

Ship Progression Path

Most players will progress through ships in the following order:

  1. Starter Raft: The basic vessel you begin with. Limited in every way but sufficient for the tutorial area.
  2. Sloop: Your first real ship upgrade. Unlocks after completing the initial port quests. Ideal for early exploration and learning naval combat basics.
  3. Brigantine: The mid-game workhorse. Unlocked after defeating the second boss or accumulating enough resources. Recommended for the majority of the game's content.
  4. Frigate: The endgame warship. Requires significant resources, rare materials from boss drops, and a large crew. Best saved for late-game challenges and fleet-scale battles.

Ship Upgrades & Customization

All ships can be upgraded and customized. Key upgrade categories include:

  • Cannon Upgrades: Increase firepower, reload speed, and unlock specialty ammunition types.
  • Hull Reinforcements: Boost armor and structural integrity. Essential for surviving boss encounters.
  • Sail Improvements: Enhance speed and turning radius. Critical for maneuverability-focused playstyles.
  • Crew Quarters: Expand crew capacity (limited by ship class maximums) and improve crew morale.
  • Cosmetics: Figureheads, sail patterns, hull paints, and lantern colors for visual personalization.
