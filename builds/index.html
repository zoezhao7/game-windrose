Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best build in Windrose?

There is no single "best" build in Windrose. The Saber + Musket DPS build offers the highest damage ceiling, but the Club + Heavy Armor tank build provides much more survivability for new players. Your ideal build depends on your playstyle and which enemies you're facing.

Are these builds final?

No. All builds on this page are theorycrafted from community research and may change with balance patches during Early Access. Windrose's meta is still evolving. Check back after major updates for revised recommendations.

Can I switch builds mid-game?

Yes. Windrose does not lock you into a permanent build. You can craft different weapons and armor sets and swap between them. Stat respec options are still being investigated, so invest stats carefully until more is known.

Which build is best for co-op?

In co-op, having one Tank (Club + Iron Armor) to draw aggro and one DPS (Rapier + Musket) to deal damage is ideal. The beginner build works well as a flexible middle-ground in any party composition.
