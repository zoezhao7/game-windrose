Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most important early recipe?

Stone Pickaxe is the key early recipe because it starts copper and clay progression.

Which recipe increases inventory space?

Torn Sailcloth Bag is the first storage upgrade, followed later by Sailor Backpack and Bosun Backpack.

Where are the full recipes?

Use the Workbench Recipes, Smelting, and crafting subcategory pages.
