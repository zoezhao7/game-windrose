Best Farming Order

Wood, stone, and plant fiber come first. Clay and copper unlock smelting. Copper tools bridge into iron. Iron and sulfur push the late resource chain for stronger tools, backpacks, and ammunition.

Frequently Asked Questions

What resource should I farm first?

Wood and stone are first, but copper and clay are the first real progression resources.

Where should I farm gunpowder early?

Use smuggler stashes and pirate camps before you can craft gunpowder from sulfur and ash.
