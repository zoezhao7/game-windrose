How We Use Patch Notes

When a patch changes crafting, enemy balance, servers, or progression, update the affected guide first, then refresh the sitemap lastmod and this news index. Avoid copying full patch notes; summarize the practical impact for players.

Perguntas Frequentes

Where should I check official Windrose updates?

Use the Steam page and official Windrose website first, then this page for guide impact summaries.

Why not mirror full patch notes?

Summaries are better for SEO and user value, and they avoid duplicating official content.
