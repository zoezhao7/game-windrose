Ashlands Preview & Building Update — New Pieces, Better Performance

Apr 30, 2026 · Preview ·Press Coverage · By PCGamesN

Windrose has been out in open waters for just over two weeks, and developer Kraken Express has already charted a course towards its next major update. The upcoming Ashlands biome is the headline feature on the horizon, but in the meantime, players are getting immediate improvements to the building system and performance.

New Building Pieces

The update adds several new building components that address community feedback about roof construction gaps and limited architectural options:

  • New roof corner pieces that eliminate the notorious "holey roof" problem
  • Additional wall variants for more creative base designs
  • Improved snapping system for smoother building placement

Performance Improvements

The performance update focuses on the foundation systems that affect all aspects of gameplay:

  • Reduced frame drops in areas with many structures
  • Improved network synchronization for co-op building
  • Better memory management for extended play sessions

Ashlands Biome Preview

While the Ashlands biome is still at least 6 months away, Kraken Express has teased new environmental hazards, unique resources, and boss encounters that will come with the expansion. Players should focus on mastering current content and preparing strong gear sets.

Guide Impact Analysis

  • Building Guide: Our Building Guide needs updating with the new roof and wall pieces.
  • Beginner Guide: The improved building system makes base construction more accessible for new players.
  • Resource Planning: No new crafting materials added — existing resource guides remain accurate.
