Voyage to Ashlands: Building & Decorating

Nov 12, 2026 · Media ·Steam Announcement · By Bambino

Ahoy, captains![p align="justify"]Our voyage to Ashlands continues! We've already explored some of the changes coming to ship customization, taken a closer look at interface improvements, and introduced armor transmogrification and dyeing. But there's still plenty more to discover.[p align="justify"]We've talked a lot about making your ship feel like your own and giving your pirate a distinctive look. But what about the place you call home? After all, even the most adventurous captain needs somewhere to return to after a long voyage.[p align="justify"]That's where building comes in.[p align="justify"]We've seen the settlements you've shared with us, from modest hideouts to elaborate estates, and we're constantly impressed by the creativity of our community. Some of your builds have left us wondering whether there's any limit to what you can accomplish with the tools already available. We're inclined to believe there isn't, but we'd still like to give you a little more to work with.[p align="justify"]For Ashlands, we've been expanding and refining our existing building sets, filling in some of the gaps you've pointed out and introducing plenty of new ways to furnish, decorate, and personalize your settlements.[p align="justify"]So, whether you're planning a cozy retreat, a bustling pirate outpost, or something considerably more ambitious, here's a look at what's coming.

Laying the Groundwork

[p align="justify"]Let's start with the building blocks themselves.[p align="justify"]Some of the feedback we've received concerns those little details that make all the difference when putting a structure together. Perhaps you have a particular roof shape in mind, but can't quite close off a corner. Or you want to finish a staircase, but none of the available railings fit the look you're going for.[p align="justify"]These might seem like small things individually, but they can make a big difference when you're trying to bring a particular design to life.[p align="justify"][p align="justify"]Roofs, corners, and finishing touches[img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/60e93615c663bc48f3ed626b22169a3fc857a335.png"][p align="justify"]We're adding a substantial collection of new roofing pieces across our building sets, giving you considerably more flexibility when constructing roofs of different shapes and sizes.[p align="justify"]New roof sections come in a variety of slopes, from gentle inclines to steeper angles, with regular and larger pieces available. We're also introducing additional corner sections, triangular roof corners, and larger corner pieces, making it easier to handle more complicated roof layouts.[p align="justify"]To help bring everything together, we're expanding the selection of roof ridges, ridge gables, and facade cornices. These pieces should make it easier to finish off a roof without awkward gaps or mismatched edges.[p align="justify"]Whether you're building a small cottage or a sprawling estate with multiple rooflines, the goal is to give you more freedom to experiment with its overall shape and silhouette.[carousel autoadvance="true"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/91a984ef507e6b5678911d0127a2442aa18c214c.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/61855463082849ad60b6bd29869f186557bb94ed.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/c481fe9fceff41bed0fcf6e5dc60cb3e39576477.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/24d12daaf701789831a71be0cd755ba3f139fb4c.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/f1d5d1de750f741bfd075b02a64815cd5fb16596.png"][p align="justify"][p align="justify"]Stairs and foundations[img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/e8358729e65c07e097f8594ce4ccaef71f88ca6b.png"][p align="justify"]Staircases are getting some attention, too. New corner steps made from twigs, planks, and logs let you change direction more naturally, while additional stone and log staircases offer more ways to match your stairs to the surrounding architecture.[p align="justify"]We're also expanding the selection of staircase railings, with new designs made from branches, logs, stone, and rope.[p align="justify"]For flooring, the Old World building set receives new mahogany and cobblestone options, available in regular, large, and triangular shapes.[p align="justify"]And for those who prefer getting straight to construction, we're introducing foundations with built-in flooring. Instead of placing a foundation and then adding a separate floor on top, you'll be able to use ready-made combinations.[p align="justify"]In the Old World set, these come with mahogany, cobblestone, parquet, or marble finishes. The Highland set also receives a foundation combining stone and wooden planks.[p align="justify"]We're rounding out the structural additions with larger beams for the Old World set, giving you more options when working on bigger buildings.[p align="justify"]Taken together, these changes should make it easier to move from your initial building idea to something that looks and feels complete.[carousel autoadvance="true"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/ad7507821af4abccbed49b74d8776fb62851a2fd.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/a17434a342e8a878e426940e8e3a8abb41b83481.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/bca22d68903fce42bf00e6c94d070ac88bca1734.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/d9452a8373f4268938a501d16d47660315f5eca1.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/e8baedcd4cfe08910182456998885e9539d61e94.png"][p align="justify"][p align="justify"]Arches, Columns, and Vaulted Ceilings[img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/89b65e2ea268c3e85e3619fdc682678cb0cbd755.png"][p align="justify"]Now, let's move on to something entirely new: arches![p align="justify"]With Ashlands, we're introducing a dedicated collection of arches, columns, and vaulted ceiling pieces to the Old World building set.[p align="justify"]Rather than offering only a handful of ready-made archways, we've approached this as a collection of architectural elements that you can combine to create your own arrangements.[p align="justify"]You can assemble columns using individual bases, shafts, and capitals, connect them with arch beams, and choose from several different opening shapes.[p align="justify"]These include pointed Gothic arches, rounded arches, half-arches, and rectangular openings. To complete the look, we're adding ceiling vaults and corner vault pieces, letting you extend the design beyond the arch itself.[p align="justify"]The pieces come in different combinations of plaster, stone, and wood, allowing you to experiment with the appearance of your structures.[p align="justify"]Perhaps you're planning an open gallery overlooking the sea, a grand entrance to your estate, or a covered passage connecting different parts of your settlement. With these pieces, you'll have more opportunities to give your buildings a distinctive architectural character.[p align="justify"]And while we're talking about entrances, the Old World set is also receiving a selection of new doors.[p align="justify"]These include smaller and regular interior doors, carved designs, stained-glass variants, double interior doors, and large double doors for more imposing entrances.[carousel autoadvance="true"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/645a28b7895bd6fdddf80e87a323f54359d315c9.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/c73dc73cfc38bc395a03dfa09da56558b37f4b35.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/2ecc46548222b9ddacf59a29ef1de859db3619e6.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/9c7e794e8256b1ac93aa9589d61e5e79cfc97ea3.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/2917415a38edf1b8c6f6180ef8ec3236ea26b878.png"]

Making a House a Home

[p align="justify"]Of course, a building is only part of the picture. Once you've raised the walls and put a roof over your head, there's still the question of what goes inside.[p align="justify"]We're expanding the selection of furniture and decorative objects in Ashlands, with a particular focus on giving different building sets more personality.[p align="justify"][p align="justify"]Swamp-inspired furniture[img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/6cb32dfd3c49abe82f1d721d243d1d048c30f94b.png"][p align="justify"]If you're fond of the swamp aesthetic, you'll have plenty of new things to experiment with.[p align="justify"]We're introducing quite a few new furniture and decoration pieces for the Swamp building set, covering everything from seating and storage to lighting and smaller decorative details.[p align="justify"]The collection includes armchairs, benches, stools, tables, wardrobes, cabinets, racks, shelving, and a folding screen. There are also shelves suspended by ropes, a bed, and various other pieces to help complete the interior.[p align="justify"]For lighting, you'll find hanging lamps, floor lamps, a table candlestick, and a wall-mounted lamp. Swamp-themed hanging ornaments in different sizes offer a few more finishing touches.[p align="justify"]Whether you're furnishing a weathered hut or creating a more elaborate interior, these additions should help you carry the swamp's distinctive atmosphere throughout your home.[carousel autoadvance="true"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/7f9ac2bc3cb368d43679afaeef0ccff3c8022630.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/3599e7a944e929d1482b8928852909a1774f3add.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/537cd937fa37fce7f31c1dfe490b4e9758008c00.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/fb4ebd7e5bbf81f27eae4dabe153c287533b1bc8.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/cd60029bf33e4a8610414a340581bfd5091465a1.png"][p align="justify"][p align="justify"]Baths, carpets, and paintings[img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/dc2738f72191538b61390bed03c48769a855ab7b.png"][p align="justify"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/62094c3777c554d306d40563d4c36f2217ad062d.png"][p align="justify"]But perhaps your ideal hideout is a little more comfortable?[p align="justify"]We're introducing a variety of new bathtubs, designed to complement the different building styles.[p align="justify"]Some feature branches and leaves, while others use old or dark wood, copper, iron, or combinations of iron and wood. From rustic wooden tubs to more refined metal designs, there are options for different kinds of interiors.[p align="justify"]And what better way to finish a room than with a carpet?[p align="justify"]We're adding a new collection of carpets, including smaller ethnic-style rugs, larger patterned carpets, and a runner. These should come in handy when decorating bedrooms, corridors, sitting rooms, and other spaces that could use a little extra warmth and character.[p align="justify"]Finally, art enthusiasts will have something to look forward to as well. The collection of paintings is growing with new seascapes, coastal and jungle landscapes, city views, still lifes, and portraits.[p align="justify"]After all, just because you're a pirate doesn't mean you can't appreciate the finer things in life.[carousel autoadvance="true"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/569e7e40e3b356d64731a69c17d2af557867394b.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/aa5b58c5e02a610a821cfb4e3715db3ab3d72e32.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/8f31a37a7428192917458a460fde62a675d18658.png"]

The Heart of Your Settlement[img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/1a102fd7dcf85d7a26e6fdbf4c0ebcf0ce8cf4fb.png"]

[p align="justify"]Every settlement needs a centre, and we wanted to give this important structure a little more character.[p align="justify"]Ashlands introduces new settlement centre designs, with indoor and outdoor variants for the Coastal, Highland, Swamp, and Old World building styles.[p align="justify"]Each follows the visual identity of its respective set, helping the settlement centre feel more at home within the architecture around it.[p align="justify"]Some of these designs also feature ambient visual effects, such as burning fire or rising smoke, adding another layer of atmosphere.[p align="justify"]It's a relatively small change in the grand scheme of things, but it should make the heart of your settlement feel more integrated with everything you've built around it.[carousel autoadvance="true"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/67026fd7f054f900f1da8549d3d17cd6ecfc05d7.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/ef32ef08a9653e0f708e7b7f38818b68b0cd1659.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/c3fb217ea45721df95e6380406aad49441871e05.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/0ff245459c599c15abe88b82ad32b6fb1c536635.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/066ce6260ebabef98039064780cca74f9184059b.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/2d892bfc0fb32641c2e946982b9ebf1fbda3b005.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/bd0692dec842ae86ecc4f86bc28349c5c06f6081.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/53d8be4fd1167ea838024923376b370a6c0f232c.png"]

More Practical Additions

higher floors, elevated platforms, or the tops of walls, and you can jump off at any point along the way.[p align="justify"]They offer an alternative to conventional staircases, especially when you're working with narrow spaces or structures where a full staircase might not fit your design.[p align="justify"][p align="justify"]Dedicated resource storage[img necessarily have to mean filling every corner with identical chests.[p align="justify"]We're introducing dedicated storage structures for wood, stone, plant fibre, and clay, giving you more visually distinct options for organizing your supplies.[p align="justify"]There's also a new storage basket for smaller items.[p align="justify"]These additions offer more ways to give your workshops, A Few More Finishing Touches

[p align="justify"]Before we wrap things up, there's another collection worth highlighting, particularly for those of you who enjoy building docks, piers, and waterfront settlements.[p align="justify"][p align="justify"]Ropes and nautical decorations[p align="justify"]We're adding a selection of decorative rope pieces that can be stretched between posts, with both straight and sagging variations.[carousel autoadvance="true"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/5b2b4a7061d18ccd9c9367ca68963031e578733f.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/8b39ed7a5760ed81b439edc57bb1eca2cfaa0735.png"][p align="justify"]There are also coils of rope and rope handrails to help complete the look.[p align="justify"]These pieces are particularly well suited to docks, mooring areas, and other nautical structures, but we're sure you'll find plenty of other creative uses for them.[p align="justify"]And to give your waterfront settlements a little more character, we're adding some decorative wooden boats, too. With both upright and overturned versions, they'll fit right in along a sandy shore, beside a wooden pier, or near a fisherman's hut. Perfect for making your docks and harbors feel a little more lived-in.[p align="justify"]Sometimes, it's the smallest details that bring an entire scene together.[carousel autoadvance="true"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/1e4067e56f57103cfec06990e7cd8a2750f13745.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/609790fb7dd764b5e5c0c6f59bc4355ff2dd0fa5.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/446acfcf900fd8dc3ecc79c370f7e85ba339e9a9.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/7f59c496ce32479ba4ef77b6ec3b27255466ded0.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/fbf836bc497d75d9265ae0a04b566eeb70576e6d.png"]
