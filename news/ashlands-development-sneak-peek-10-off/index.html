Ashlands Development Sneak Peek + 10% Off!

Aug 28, 2026 · Preview ·Steam Announcement · By Bambino

Hey, captains!The end of summer is drawing near, and while it’s a little sad to see it go, we’re putting more and more effort into our next update, Ashlands.We decided to share a few small work-in-progress snippets from development to cheer you up a bit. The team sends its warmest regards and can’t wait to show you more of what we’ve been working on!Starting off with a colorful new armor set for one of our NPCs. It should be pretty obvious who this is, right? [img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/bbad2d523cb78bc52d626e12065377f4cf8c50f6.jpg"]Some love for the Halberds and Greatswords — existing weapons deserve it, too! [img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/2ad90085e89b2c021f360c8e17ba74fb34972943.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/f754da73effec7c575c97df5e4b0a758286108ca.png"]Some faction clothing sets — to better reflect their character. Can you guess which set belongs to which faction?[img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/54cea22eefac5c492ab4c47a1260458799847396.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/f05fa02665881b675f1ee4a87e97f2617b267db4.png"]And to top it all off, here’s a look at a Brethren of the Coast Galleon variant, along with a little preview of our ship damage FX!The Galleon and other ships are still very much a work in progress, so the final lineup may change as development continues — but we’re excited to give you this little glimpse of what’s taking shape![img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/f2aeea4e4227dc9c24595bd1c701f37596b8238b.png"][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/867f15b51e0e2b4a38b95b5a53b28e177562dbaf.png"]And while we’re here, we’d also like to share that we’re taking part in Steam PvE Survival Crafting Fest 2026, running from August 31 to September 7!Our 10% discount is already live and will remain available until the end of the Fest, so now’s a great time to jump aboard!Until next time, crew!
