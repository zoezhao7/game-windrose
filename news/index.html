  • April 14, 2026 Official

    Windrose Launches in Early Access — 1 Million Sales in Week One

    Windrose officially launched in Steam Early Access on April 14, 2026, published by Pocketpair Publishing. Within the first week, the game surpassed 1 million copies sold, making it one of the biggest EA launches of the year. The game peaked at Steam Wishlist #6 prior to launch. Set in a pirate-themed survival open world with 3 ecological zones, 30+ islands, 3 buildable ships, and 90+ dungeons, Windrose blends base-building, Soulslite combat, and naval warfare into a 50-70 hour main campaign.
