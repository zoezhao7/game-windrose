Windrose x Youtooz

Sep 26, 2026 · Media ·Steam Announcement · By Bambino

Get your collection shelves ready! We’re happy to announce our collaboration with Youtooz on the first-ever Windrose collectibles![p align="justify"]Our first thematic drop features three things every Archipelago captain knows all too well: the Dodo plushie, the Boar plushie, and the Coin pin.[img src="{STEAM_CLAN_IMAGE}/45546879/b4ec54d8f025a0a7a91d63ff3aa1c7862cf4149d.png"][p align="justify"]Interested? Here’s how it works:

  • Voting is open for 7 days starting now. Your votes will decide whether each concept gets made, with at least 200 votes required for it to move forward.
    • Voting schedule: September 25 – October 2, 3PM EDT / 12PM PDT / 9PM CEST[/*]
    [/*]
  • Everyone who votes will receive an additional $2.99 discount off the final price if the item goes into production.[/*]
  • If a concept reaches its voting goal, Youtooz will also hold a giveaway: 5 items from each successful concept will be given away among those who participated in the voting.[/*]
  • Like what you see? Share it with your friends and fellow captains to help your favorite concept reach the goal![/*]
[p align="justify"]Rules apply (Terms of Use, Shipping Policy). To vote and learn more, please visit:
  • https://youtooz.com/products/truffle-the-combat-boar-plush-9-inch[/*]
  • https://youtooz.com/products/dodo-plush-9-inch[/*]
  • https://youtooz.com/products/pirate-coin-pin[/*]
[p align="justify"]Hurry — this treasure map won’t be around for long.[p align="justify"]And a huge thanks to our friends at Youtooz for making this possible!
