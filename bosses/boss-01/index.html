General Early-Game Boss Tips

  • Stock up on Bandages — craft at least 10-15 before attempting any boss
  • Upgrade to Copper weapons — Stone weapons deal minimal damage to bosses
  • Learn dodge timing — press Ctrl just before an attack lands for invincibility frames
  • Manage stamina — never deplete your bar completely; always save enough for one dodge
  • Watch for tells — every boss attack has a wind-up animation; learn to recognize them

FAQ

When is the first boss fight?

The first boss is encountered in the starter area after completing initial island quests and crafting Copper-tier equipment.

What gear level is recommended?

Minimum: full Copper tools and weapons. At least one melee weapon (Saber recommended) and 10-15 Bandages.

Can I fight bosses in co-op?

Yes! Windrose supports 1-8 player co-op. Bosses scale with player count, but having teammates to split aggro makes fights significantly easier.
