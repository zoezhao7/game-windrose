Windrose Grave of Jaime Lopez: Location, Lore & Expedition Story (2026)

The Grave of Jaime Lopez ("Old Grave — Jaime Lopez" in some patches) is the second of four expedition graves in Windrose's hidden Secret of the Expedition lore chain. It is not a crafting item or a drop — it's a readable story object the captain narrates aloud, filling in the back-story behind the High Priestess fight in the Cursed Swamps.

What the Grave Says

The captain reads the inscription aloud:

"Jaime… Jaime… I pulled you out of a pit of debt and made you my first mate. And you repaid me by conspiring with the enemy — betraying the Order, betraying the faith."

This is the captain's monologue at the grave of his former first mate. Three pieces of context are confirmed from this single inscription:

  • Jaime Lopez was the captain's first mate, hired out of debt and elevated to second-in-command of the original expedition.
  • Jaime betrayed "the Order" — the same Order whose surviving members appear later as enemies in the Cursed Swamps.
  • The captain personally killed (or ordered killed) Jaime, foreshadowing his ruthless streak and tying directly into the High Priestess confrontation.

Where to Find the Grave

The grave is located in the Cursed Swamps biome along the approach path to the High Priestess arena. You will pass it naturally on the main quest route — there is no hidden trigger, but it is easy to walk past without interacting.

  • Step 1. Complete the Israel Hands fight to unlock travel to the Cursed Swamps.
  • Step 2. Follow the swamp path north from the dock until you reach the ruined expedition camp.
  • Step 3. The grave is one of four marked stone markers near the camp. Look for the second one in story order — the captain will speak the inscription as soon as you interact.

Note: exact in-game coordinates shift between patches. The marker lies on the main quest path, so as long as you stay on the road to the priestess you will encounter it.

The Full Expedition Chain (4 Graves + 1 Skeleton)

Jaime's grave is the second of four story markers. Reading all four unlocks the full back-story for the High Priestess encounter:

Secret of the Expedition — Reading Order
# Marker Reveals
1 Grave of Esteban Vargas The captain's first lost crewmate; sets the mournful tone.
2 Grave of Jaime Lopez (this page) Reveals the betrayal — Jaime conspired against the Order.
3 Grave of Guillermo Sanchez Davy Jones is named; the priestess medallion is introduced as a weapon against the dead.
4 Skeleton of Juan de la Cosa Confirms the medallion works but the original expedition was overrun. Sets up the High Priestess fight.

How This Connects to the High Priestess Fight

The expedition graves explain why the captain — your character — is hunting the High Priestess. The Order, Jaime's betrayal, and the priestess medallion are all setup for that boss arena. If you've found Jaime's grave but skipped the others, double back to read them before the fight; the priestess's dialogue makes more sense once you know what the original expedition was carrying.

For the actual fight mechanics, gear checks, and drops, see the High Priestess boss guide.

FAQ

Is "Old Grave" the same as "Grave of Jaime Lopez"?

Yes. Players sometimes search for "Windrose old grave Jaime Lopez" because earlier patches and some loading-screen captions refer to the markers as "old graves." The in-game item name is Grave of Jaime Lopez and it is tagged as a Legendary lore item in the database.

Does the Grave of Jaime Lopez drop anything?

No items, no XP, no currency. It is a pure lore item — interaction triggers a captain monologue that updates your journal entry for the Secret of the Expedition.

Can I read the grave more than once?

Yes. The captain's voice line plays every time you interact, and the journal entry remains in your codex permanently after the first read.

Is finding the grave required for any achievement?

Tracking all four expedition markers is tied to a hidden codex completion entry. It is not gated behind a story-progress achievement, but completionists should grab all four on a single Cursed Swamps run.
