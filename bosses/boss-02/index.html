Mid-Game Boss Preparation

  • Iron-tier weapons recommended — if available before the fight
  • Bring ranged backup — Musket or Blunderbuss for phases with distance mechanics
  • Craft Combat Repair Kit — 3 Wooden Plank + 1 Rum Bottle + 1 Steel Nails
  • Upgrade to Sailor Backpack — more inventory = more healing supplies

FAQ

How does the second boss differ from the first?

The second boss introduces multi-phase mechanics, environmental hazards, and requires better stamina management. Expect more complex attack patterns compared to the starter boss.

Do I need Iron gear for the second boss?

While Copper gear is viable with skill, Iron weapons significantly reduce the fight duration. At minimum, bring Copper-tier weapons and plenty of healing.
