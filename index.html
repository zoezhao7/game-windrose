What is Windrose?

Windrose (also known as Windrose: Pirate Survival RPG) is a pirate-themed survival adventure developed by Kraken Express and co-published with Pocketpair Publishing. Released into Early Access on April 14, 2026, the game blends classic survival crafting with soulslite combat — a lighter take on Soulslike mechanics that rewards timing, positioning, and stamina management without being overly punishing.

The game takes place across a procedurally generated open-sea archipelago where players can explore islands, gather resources, build bases, craft hundreds of items, and sail between locations on ships they construct themselves. Windrose supports solo play and co-op multiplayer for up to 10 players, making it ideal for both lone adventurers and organized crews.

Core Gameplay Systems

Windrose combines five interconnected systems that define its gameplay loop. Each system feeds into the others — resources fuel crafting, crafting equips your character, and better gear opens new bosses and islands.

🔨
Crafting & Building

Over 950 items across multiple tiers of crafting stations. From Workbench tools to Weaponsmith weapons — build your base anywhere with a free-form system.

⚔️
Soulslite Combat

Dodge-roll, perfect block, and stamina management form the core. Boss encounters feature multi-phase attack patterns that demand practice.

⛵
Sailing & Ship Building

Construct and customize ships from Sloop to Frigate class. Navigate dynamic wind systems, manage cargo, and engage in naval combat.

⛏️
Resource Gathering

Mine copper, iron, clay, and gunpowder from cave deposits and surface nodes. Each resource has specific island biomes where it spawns.

📈
Progression & Talents

Level up by clearing POIs and completing quests. Allocate talent points across Toughguy, Swashbuckler, and Mariner trees with free respecs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Windrose free to play?

No. Windrose is a paid Early Access title available on Steam. The current price is approximately $29.99 USD. There is no free-to-play version, and the game does not include microtransactions.

Can I play Windrose solo or is it multiplayer only?

Windrose supports both solo play and co-op multiplayer for up to 10 players. The entire game can be completed solo, though some boss encounters are significantly easier with a crew. You can host a private world or join dedicated servers.

How often is Windrose updated?

During Early Access, Kraken Express releases patches approximately every 1-2 weeks. Major content updates arrive monthly. The current version is Patch 0.4.2 (May 2026). Check our News page for the latest patch notes.

What are the PC system requirements for Windrose?

Minimum: Windows 10, Intel i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600, 8 GB RAM, NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD RX 580, 20 GB storage. Recommended: Windows 10/11, Intel i7-10700 / AMD Ryzen 7 3700X, 16 GB RAM, NVIDIA RTX 2060 / AMD RX 6600 XT, SSD with 20 GB free space.

Is Windrose similar to Valheim or Palworld?

Windrose shares the survival-crafting loop with Valheim and the creature/open-world elements with Palworld, but differentiates itself with a pirate nautical theme, soulslite combat (dodge-rolling and perfect blocking), and deep ship building and naval combat systems. If you enjoy Valheim's exploration and crafting but want more action-oriented combat on the high seas, Windrose is a strong choice.
