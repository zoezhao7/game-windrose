About Windrose Guides

What Is Windrose Guides?

Windrose Guides is a community-maintained, unofficial wiki and strategy guide for Windrose, the pirate-themed survival RPG developed by Kraken Express and published by Pocketpair Publishing. We provide comprehensive, up-to-date information on crafting recipes, resource locations, boss strategies, ship building, weapon stats, and everything else you need to survive and thrive on the high seas.

Our Mission

We built this site to serve the Windrose community with:

  • Accurate, verified data — Every recipe, resource location, and boss mechanic is tested in-game.
  • SEO-friendly, AI-ready content — Structured data and clear formatting so search engines and AI assistants can surface answers quickly.
  • Mobile-first access — All pages work great on phones and tablets for quick reference while you play.
  • Up-to-date information — We track Early Access patches and update content as the game evolves.

Important Disclaimers

⚠️ Not Official

Windrose Guides is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Kraken Express or Pocketpair Publishing. All game-related trademarks, copyrights, and assets are the property of their respective owners. This is a fan-made, unofficial resource created by and for the Windrose community.

⚠️ Early Access Data

Windrose launched in Steam Early Access on April 14, 2026. Game mechanics, recipes, stats, and content are subject to change as Kraken Express continues development. While we strive to keep information accurate, numbers and requirements may become outdated between patches. Always verify critical information in-game or via official sources.

⚠️ Data Accuracy

Information on this site is sourced from a combination of in-game testing, official developer communications, verified community contributions, and published guides from trusted community partners. Despite our best efforts, errors can occur. If you spot incorrect information, please contact us so we can fix it.

How We Source Data

  • In-game testing: Recipes, resource spawns, and boss mechanics are verified directly in-game.
  • Official sources: Steam announcements, developer Discord updates, and official patch notes.
  • Community contributions: Corrections and tips submitted by Windrose players.
  • Community guides: Cross-referenced with published guides from Mobalytics, MMOGAH, and other trusted gaming resources.

Content Coverage

Windrose Guides currently covers:

  • Beginner Guide — Day 1 to Day 10 walkthrough, difficulty selection, and survival basics.
  • Crafting Recipes — Complete Workbench recipes for Lv1, Lv2, and Lv3, plus Smelting Furnace processing.
  • Resource Locations — Copper, Iron, Clay, Gunpowder, and more with detailed acquisition guides.
  • Boss Strategies — Boss mechanics, preparation tips, and phase-by-phase tactics.
  • Ship Building — Complete guide to Sloop, Brigantine, and Frigate with stat comparisons.
  • Weapons & Builds — Melee and ranged weapon stats, armor sets, and tier lists.
  • FAQ — Top 20 most-asked Windrose questions with concise answers.

Contributing

Windrose Guides is maintained by community volunteers. If you'd like to contribute corrections, new data, or guide content, we welcome your help. Visit our Contact page to reach out. All contributions are reviewed before publication to maintain accuracy standards.
