Windrose Clay Guide: Locations, Farming & Crafting Recipes (2026)

Clay is one of the first resources you should gather in Windrose. It is a deceptively important material—without enough Clay, you cannot build the Charcoal Kiln or the Smelting Furnace, which means you cannot smelt any ore into ingots. This guide covers where to find Clay, how to farm it, the exact amount you need, and every Clay recipe.

How to Get Clay

Clay gathering is straightforward and available from the very start of the game:

  1. Explore riverbanks, lakeshores, and coastal edges on the starting island.
  2. Look for dark mud patches — brown-black colored ground spots near water. These are Clay deposits.
  3. Use your Stone Pickaxe (3 Stone + 3 Wood at Workbench Lv1) on the mud patch.
  4. Collect the Clay — each successful swing yields Clay. Multiple deposits can be found along a single riverbank.

📜 How Much Clay Do You Need?

The two mandatory Clay structures require 35 Clay total:

  • Charcoal Kiln: 25 Wood + 20 Clay — produces Charcoal from Wood
  • Smelting Furnace: 15 Clay + 30 Stone — smelts ore into ingots

Budget an additional 10–20 Clay for Clay Pots (storage) and Clay Bottles (alchemy/potion containers). A safe early-game target is 50–60 Clay. Since deposits respawn, you can always gather more later.

FAQ

Where is the best spot to farm Clay?

The starting island's riverbanks and shorelines are the best early-game spots. Look for large dark mud patches. The same deposits respawn over time.

Can I collect Clay without a Pickaxe?

No, you need at least a Stone Pickaxe (3 Stone + 3 Wood at Workbench). Higher-tier pickaxes work faster.

How much Clay do I need total?

Minimum 35 Clay for the Charcoal Kiln (20) and Smelting Furnace (15). Budget 50-60 total for pots and bottles.
