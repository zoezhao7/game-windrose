Windrose Iron Ore Guide: Foothills Mining Locations & Recipes (2026)

Iron Ore marks your transition from early-game survival into mid-game dominance. With Foothills Iron Ingots, you unlock the full Lv2 Workbench—the Anvil, Iron-grade tools, firearms ammunition, and heavy armor. This guide covers how to reach the Foothills, iron vein locations, smelting ratios, and every Lv2 recipe.

How to Get Iron Ore

  1. Reach the Foothills region — progress the main questline northward from the Coastal Jungle. The terrain transitions from jungle to rocky foothills.
  2. Craft a Copper Pickaxe (2 Copper Ingots + 3 Wood at Workbench) — this is mandatory. The Stone Pickaxe cannot mine Iron Ore veins.
  3. Explore rocky slopes for Iron Ore veins — they appear as dark metallic streaks on exposed rock surfaces throughout the Foothills.
  4. Mine with your Copper Pickaxe — each vein drops Iron Ore. Collect as much as you can carry.
  5. Smelt into Foothills Iron Ingots — return to your base and place 6 Iron Ore + 2 Charcoal into the Smelting Furnace for 1 Foothills Iron Ingot.

Iron Ore Smelting: Higher Fuel Cost

Iron smelting is more Charcoal-intensive than Copper. The ratio is 6 Iron Ore + 2 Charcoal = 1 Foothills Iron Ingot—twice the Charcoal cost per ingot compared to Copper. Before your first Iron smelting run, ensure you have a healthy Charcoal stockpile. Keep the Charcoal Kiln running with spare Wood to avoid downtime.

Iron Pickaxe: Gateway to Sulfur & Gunpowder

The Iron Pickaxe is your most important Iron craft. It is the only tool capable of mining Sulfur deposits—the key ingredient for late-game Gunpowder production. Craft the Iron Pickaxe as soon as you have spare Foothills Iron Ingots. Without it, Sulfur nodes remain untouchable.

💡 Pro Tips for Iron Mining

  • Bring extra Copper Pickaxes or repair materials. Iron Ore veins are tougher than Copper deposits and drain pickaxe durability faster.
  • Foothills enemies hit harder. Upgrade to at least Copper armor before your first Iron run. Bring healing food.
  • Iron veins are scattered, not clustered. Unlike Copper caves, Iron is found on open rocky surfaces. Explore thoroughly—veins can be hidden behind rock formations.
  • Smelt Iron in bulk batches. With the 2 Charcoal per ingot cost, running the Furnace for single ingots is inefficient. Mine 30+ Iron Ore per trip.
  • Prioritize the Anvil first, then the Iron Pickaxe for Sulfur access, then armor for survivability.

What Iron Unlocks Next

  • Iron Pickaxe → unlocks Gunpowder crafting via Sulfur mining.
  • Anvil → unlocks Lv2 weapons and armor recipes at the Workbench.
  • Iron Bullets → ammunition for firearms (combined with Gunpowder).
  • Explorer Backpack → maximum inventory capacity for extended expeditions.
  • Iron Armor Set → survivability for harder biomes and upcoming Boss fights.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find Iron Ore in Windrose?

Iron Ore is found in the Foothills region, accessed after progressing the main questline northward from the Coastal Jungle. Look for Iron Ore veins—dark metallic streaks on rocky surfaces—throughout the Foothills. Veins are scattered on exposed rock, not concentrated in caves like Copper.

What pickaxe do I need for Iron Ore?

You need a Copper Pickaxe (2 Copper Ingots + 3 Wood at Workbench). The Stone Pickaxe cannot mine Iron Ore. Smelt Copper Ore first, craft the Copper Pickaxe, then head to the Foothills.

What is the smelting ratio for Iron Ore?

The Iron smelting ratio is 6 Iron Ore + 2 Charcoal = 1 Foothills Iron Ingot. This is twice the Charcoal cost of Copper smelting (which uses 1 Charcoal). Stockpile extra Charcoal before starting Iron smelting batches.

What can I craft with Foothills Iron Ingots?

Foothills Iron Ingots unlock all Lv2 Workbench recipes: the Anvil (station upgrade), Iron Pickaxe, Iron Axe, Iron Sword, Iron Armor pieces (Chestplate, Leggings, Helmet), Iron Bullets (ammunition), and the Explorer Backpack (largest inventory upgrade).

Do I need to build the Anvil to use Iron Ingots?

Some basic Iron tools (Pickaxe, Axe) can be crafted directly at the Workbench. However, the Anvil (3 Foothills Iron Ingot + 5 Stone) is required to unlock Lv2 weapons and armor recipes. Build it early in your Iron progression.

How many Iron Ore veins are in the Foothills?

The Foothills region contains 15–25 Iron Ore veins scattered across rocky terrain. Veins are spread out rather than clustered, so thorough exploration is required. Veins respawn over time, making the Foothills a renewable Iron source.
