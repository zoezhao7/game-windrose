Windrose Rare & Advanced Materials Guide (2026)

Beyond the basic resources like Copper and Iron, Windrose features several rare and advanced materials critical for mid-to-late game progression. This guide covers where to find them and what they're used for.

Hardwood

An advanced wood type required for Lv3 Workbench crafting (Timber) and high-tier items.

How to Get

  • Cut down large trees with a Copper or Iron Axe in later biomes (Foothills+)
  • Not available on the starter island

Used In

  • Timber (3 Hardwood) — Lv3 Workbench
  • Simple Fishing Rod (5 Hardwood + 3 Rope + 2 Foothills Iron Ingot)
  • Advanced building recipes

Tanned Leather

Upgraded leather material needed for the highest-tier backpack.

How to Get

  • Process Rough Hide at a Tannery or advanced crafting station
  • May require specific Foothills-region recipes

Used In

  • Bosun Backpack: 1 Sailor Backpack + 5 Tanned Leather + 2 Foothills Iron Ingot

Rum Bottles

Used in advanced repair kits. Found alongside Gunpowder in smuggler stashes.

How to Get

  • Smuggler's Treasure chest (4 bottles alongside gunpowder)
  • Pirate camp loot crates
  • Shipwreck debris on beaches

Used In

  • Combat Repair Kit: 3 Wooden Plank + 1 Rum Bottle + 1 Steel Nails
  • Master Combat Repair Kit: 2 Timber + 5 Rum Bottle + 3 Steel Nails

Steel Nails

Advanced nails for high-tier repair kits.

How to Get

  • Crafted from Foothills Iron Ingot at Lv2 Workbench
  • Loot from advanced pirate camps and shipwrecks

Used In

  • Combat Repair Kit
  • Master Combat Repair Kit

Rough Hide

Basic leather from early-game animals.

How to Get

  • Kill Boars on the starter island
  • Other wildlife in coastal areas

Used In

  • Survivor's Boots: 2 Rough Hide + 2 Coarse Fabric
  • Sailor Backpack: 1 Torn Sailcloth Bag + 5 Rough Hide + 2 Copper Ingot

FAQ

Where can I find Hardwood?

Hardwood comes from larger, darker trees found in the Foothills biome and beyond. The starter island only has regular Wood trees — you need an Iron Axe to harvest Hardwood efficiently.

How do I get Tanned Leather?

Tanned Leather is processed from Rough Hide at a Tannery station (requires Foothills progression). As more players reach mid-game, the exact recipe will be confirmed.

Where can I farm Rum Bottles?

The best source is pirate camps and smuggler stashes. Clear camps, loot all crates, and revisit after they reset. Shipwreck debris on beaches also occasionally contains Rum Bottles.
