Windrose Resource Locations Guide: Copper, Iron, Clay & More (2026)

Resources are the backbone of your survival journey in Windrose. From your first Stone Pickaxe to crafting Gunpowder for firearms, knowing where to find every resource is the difference between smooth sailing and getting stuck. This guide covers all core resources with exact locations, required tools, and crafting uses—updated for 2026.

Resource Progression Path

Windrose follows a clear resource tier system. Here is the optimal progression path:

  1. Stone Pickaxe (3 Stone + 3 Wood at Workbench Lv1) — your first mining tool.
  2. Clay — gather 20+ Clay from riverbanks for the Smelting Furnace (15 Clay + 30 Stone) and Charcoal Kiln (25 Wood + 20 Clay).
  3. Copper Ore — mine from Coastal Jungle caves, smelt 6 Ore + 1 Charcoal into Copper Ingots.
  4. Copper Pickaxe — crafted from Copper Ingots at Workbench Lv1. Required for Iron mining.
  5. Iron Ore — reach Foothills, mine Iron veins with Copper Pickaxe, smelt into Foothills Iron Ingots.
  6. Iron Pickaxe — crafted from Foothills Iron Ingots at Workbench Lv2. Required for Sulfur mining.
  7. Sulfur + Ash — combine at Millstone for renewable Gunpowder crafting.

Smelting Furnace: Your First Major Craft

The Smelting Furnace is the gateway to metal tools and advanced crafting. It requires 15 Clay + 30 Stone and must be built before you can smelt any ore. Alongside it, craft the Charcoal Kiln (25 Wood + 20 Clay) to produce Charcoal—the fuel needed to run the furnace. Without these two structures, no Ore can become an Ingot.

Hidden & Missable Resources

  • Gunpowder (Smuggler's Treasure): On the first Coastal Jungle island, look for a "Smuggler's Treasure" POI. Break the wood pile to find stairs leading down. Defeat the Drowned enemy and open the chest for 10 Gunpowder + 4 Rum Bottles.
  • Fast Travel Bell (Supply Cache): Found in a supply cache in the Coastal Jungle. This secret item lets you teleport home instantly — incredibly valuable for saving time.
  • Boar Whistle (Foothills Altar): Make an offering (Ritual Oil + Meat) at the altar in the Foothills to receive this secret item that summons a boar companion.
  • Pearls (Scallop Shells): Right-click scallop shells dropped by beach enemies to extract pearls — they work as emergency ammunition when you're out of gunpowder.
  • Fertile Soil + Corn (Ancient Farm): Found at Ancient Farm POIs in the Foothills. These unlock passive food production via Seedbeds at your base.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most important resource in Windrose?

Copper Ore is the most critical early-game resource. It unlocks Copper tools, Copper Nails for storage expansion, and the entire metal tool progression chain. Your first priority after crafting a Stone Pickaxe should be locating a Copper Deposit Mine—marked by a pickaxe icon on the map in the Coastal Jungle biome.

Which pickaxe do I need for each ore?

Stone Pickaxe mines Clay and Copper Ore. Copper Pickaxe is required for Iron Ore. Iron Pickaxe is required for Sulfur. Each ore tier demands the pickaxe one tier below it—you cannot skip ahead.

How many resources do I need for the Smelting Furnace?

The Smelting Furnace costs 15 Clay + 30 Stone. You should also craft the Charcoal Kiln (25 Wood + 20 Clay) at the same time, as the Kiln produces the Charcoal fuel needed to run the Furnace. Budget at least 35 Clay total for both structures.

Can I get Gunpowder without crafting it?

Yes. In the early game, Gunpowder is found in the Smuggler’s Treasure cave on the first island (10 Gunpowder + 4 Rum in a chest behind breakable wood piles guarded by Drowned enemies). Pirate Camps may also drop Gunpowder as loot. Late-game crafting at the Millstone provides a renewable source.

What should I gather first when starting Windrose?

Start with Wood and Stone to craft a Stone Pickaxe and Stone Axe. Then gather Clay from dark mud patches near water on the starting island—you need 35+ Clay for the Kiln and Furnace. After those structures are built, head to a Copper Deposit Mine for Copper Ore.
