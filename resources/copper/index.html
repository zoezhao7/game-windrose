Windrose Copper Ore Guide: Mining Locations & Recipes (2026)

Copper Ore is your gateway to the metal age in Windrose. It transforms your primitive stone tools into durable copper equipment and unlocks expanded storage, fast travel, and advanced bags. This guide covers exact copper deposit locations, smelting ratios, cave dangers, and every copper recipe you need.

How to Get Copper Ore

  1. Check your map — look for a pickaxe icon (⚒). This marks a Copper Deposit Mine in the Coastal Jungle biome.
  2. Travel to the cave entrance — bring a Stone Pickaxe, several torches, a weapon (sword or axe), and food for healing.
  3. Enter the cave — defeat any Drowned enemies lurking inside before you start mining. They are aggressive melee attackers and will interrupt your mining.
  4. Mine Poor Copper Ore deposits — use your Stone Pickaxe on the copper-colored rock deposits scattered throughout the cave.
  5. Smelt into Copper Ingots — return to base and place 6 Copper Ore + 1 Charcoal into your Smelting Furnace to produce 1 Copper Ingot.

⚠ Cave Dangers

Drowned enemies lurk inside every Copper Deposit Mine. They are hostile, melee-range attackers that can quickly overwhelm an unprepared player. Always bring torches (caves are pitch dark), a weapon to clear enemies, and food to heal between fights. Clear the cave of Drowned enemies before you start mining—getting interrupted mid-swing wastes time and durability.

The Smelting Furnace: Required Before Smelting

Before you can turn Copper Ore into Copper Ingots, you must craft two essential structures at your Workbench:

💡 Pro Tips for Copper Mining

  • Clear the cave first, then mine. Drowned enemies respawn slowly, so clearing them gives you uninterrupted mining time.
  • Each cave yields 8–15 Poor Copper Ore deposits. With the 6:1 smelting ratio, expect 1–3 Copper Ingots per cave run. Multiple trips are normal.
  • Prioritize the Copper Pickaxe first. It unlocks Iron Ore mining, which is the next tier. Then craft the Sailor Backpack for extra inventory space on future runs.
  • Mark the cave entrance on your map for easy return trips. Deposits respawn after some time, letting you farm the same cave repeatedly.
  • Bring extra Charcoal. Starting a smelt batch and running out of fuel is frustrating. Stockpile Charcoal at the Kiln before smelting.

What Copper Unlocks Next

With Copper Ingots flowing, your next goals are:

  • Copper Pickaxe → unlocks Iron Ore mining in the Foothills.
  • Sailor Backpack → more inventory space for longer resource expeditions.
  • Copper Nails → expanded storage chests at your base.
  • Fast Travel Bell → quick return to base from distant resource locations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find Copper Ore in Windrose?

Copper Ore is found in Copper Deposit Mines marked by a pickaxe icon (⚒) on the map. These caves are located in the Coastal Jungle biome. Open your map, spot the pickaxe symbol, and travel to that location. Enter the cave with a Stone Pickaxe, torches, and a weapon.

How do I smelt Copper Ore into Copper Ingots?

Place 6 Copper Ore + 1 Charcoal into the Smelting Furnace to produce 1 Copper Ingot. You must build the Smelting Furnace (15 Clay + 30 Stone) and Charcoal Kiln (25 Wood + 20 Clay) first. The Kiln produces Charcoal from Wood, which fuels the Furnace.

What pickaxe do I need to mine Copper Ore?

You need the Stone Pickaxe (3 Stone + 3 Wood at Workbench Lv1). It is the first pickaxe you can craft and works on both Clay and Copper Ore. A Copper Pickaxe or better also works, but by then you already have copper access.

Are there enemies inside Copper Deposit Mines?

Yes. Drowned enemies inhabit Copper Deposit Mines. They are hostile melee attackers. Bring a weapon (sword or axe), torches for visibility, and food for healing. Clear all enemies before mining to avoid being attacked mid-swing.

How many Copper Ore deposits are in one cave?

Each Copper Deposit Mine contains 8 to 15 Poor Copper Ore deposits. With the 6:1 smelting ratio, one full cave clear yields roughly 1–3 Copper Ingots. You will need to visit multiple caves or re-visit after respawn to craft a full set of copper tools.

Do Copper Ore deposits respawn?

Yes, Copper Ore deposits inside mines do respawn after a period of in-game time. Mark the cave entrance on your map so you can return for repeat farming runs. Respawning makes it possible to farm Copper indefinitely from the same mine.
