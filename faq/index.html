Windrose FAQ — Top 20 Frequently Asked Questions (2026)

Got a question about Windrose? You're not alone. Below are the 20 most frequently asked questions we see from new and returning players — covering crafting, resources, combat mechanics, ships, and survival tips. Each answer is kept concise and actionable so you can get back to sailing.

All Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I get copper in Windrose?

Find the small pickaxe icon on your map to locate Copper Deposit Mines (caves). Enter the cave, use a Stone Pickaxe to mine Poor Copper Ore, then bring it back to your Smelting Furnace. Recipe: 6 Copper Ore + 1 Charcoal = 1 Copper Ingot. Caves often contain Drowned enemies — bring weapons and bandages.

Related: Copper Ore Guide — Locations, Uses & Tips

2. How do I upgrade the Workbench to Level 2?

Build a Sawhorse (20 Wood + 10 Copper Ingot) near your Workbench within the same Bonfire radius. The Sawhorse add-on triggers the Lv2 upgrade automatically. For Lv3, build a Toolbox (10 Wood + 20 Nails + 5 Foothills Iron Ingot) near the Workbench. Some recipes also require you to pick up new materials before they appear in the crafting menu.

Related: Workbench Crafting Recipes — All Lv1/Lv2/Lv3

3. How do I get my first ship?

Complete the starting island quests. After you build the Weaponsmith Workshop and craft your first melee weapon (Saber, Rapier, or Club), you'll receive your first ship for free and unlock the "Rescuing the Crew" and "I Need a Bigger Boat" quest chains.

Related: Ship Building Guide — Sloop, Brigantine & Frigate

4. How do I fast travel?

Craft a Fast Travel Bell at the Workbench: 10 Copper Ingot + 3 Rope. Place it at your base or any location you want to return to. You can teleport between any placed bells. Rope is crafted from 3 Plant Fiber at the Workbench.

5. How do I get gunpowder early?

The fastest early-game method: on the first Coastal Jungle island, find the Smuggler's Treasure point. Break the wood pile, go downstairs, defeat the Drowned enemy, and open the chest on the right — it contains 10 Gunpowder + 4 Rum Bottles. Also clear Blackbeard Pirate Camps for gunpowder drops.

Later, craft it: 10 Sulfur + 20 Ash = 10 Gunpowder at a Millstone (requires reaching Foothills and an Iron Pickaxe for Sulfur).

Related: Gunpowder Guide — Early & Late-Game Sources

6. Where do I find clay?

Clay spawns randomly on the map, common on the starting island. Look for large dark soil patches near water or riverbanks. Mine it with a Stone Pickaxe. Used for Clay Pots, Clay Bottles, Charcoal Kiln (25 Wood + 20 Clay), and Smelting Furnace (15 Clay + 30 Stone).

Related: Clay Locations & Uses Guide

7. How do I craft a bigger backpack?

Three backpack tiers, crafted at the Workbench:

  1. Torn Sailcloth Bag (Lv1): 2 Coarse Fabric + 1 Rope
  2. Sailor Backpack (Lv2): 1 Torn Sailcloth Bag + 5 Rough Hide + 2 Copper Ingot
  3. Bosun Backpack (Lv3): 1 Sailor Backpack + 5 Tanned Leather + 2 Foothills Iron Ingot

Coarse Fabric = 3 Plant Fiber. Rope = 3 Plant Fiber. Both at the Workbench.

8. What's the best difficulty for beginners?

Calm Waters — reduces enemy damage and stress, perfect for first-time players. If you have survival game experience (Rust, Valheim, etc.), choose High Seas for the standard 100% experience. Storm's Edge increases enemy health/damage for challenge seekers. Captain's Choice offers custom sliders up to 500%. Difficulty is locked at world creation.

9. Can I pause the game?

No! Windrose has no pause function at all. Always ensure your surroundings are completely safe before opening menus or inventory. If you need to step away, return to your base or a cleared area first.

10. How do I heal?

Craft Bandages at the Workbench: 1 Coarse Fabric each. Always carry 5-10 bandages and use them before health gets critical. Coarse Fabric = 3 Plant Fiber. For passive healing, eat coconuts (from palm trees) — they restore both hunger and thirst.

11. How many players can play co-op?

The game supports up to 10 players, but the developers recommend 2–4 players for the best experience. Groups of 5–8 may experience performance dips, and 10-player mode is considered experimental. Crew members can operate different ship weapons during naval combat, or split up to explore islands and dungeons together.

12. What are pearls used for?

Right-click on scallop shells dropped by beach enemies — pearls work as emergency ammunition when you run out of gunpowder. They're a useful backup in the early game before you have a steady ammo supply.

13. How do I get more inventory space?

Craft backpack upgrades: Torn Sailcloth Bag → Sailor Backpack (Lv2 Workbench) → Bosun Backpack (Lv3 Workbench). Each tier gives significantly more carrying capacity. See FAQ #7 for full crafting recipes.

14. Can I change difficulty mid-game?

No. Difficulty is locked permanently when you create your world. Choose carefully. To change difficulty, you must create a new world.

15. What happens when I die?

You drop your items at your death location. You can return to recover them. A key advantage: enemies do not regenerate health when they lose aggro, so you can use hit-and-run tactics against tough opponents.

16. How do I build a base?

Press B to enter build mode, Q for fine-tune placement, V for overhead view. You can build almost anywhere. Tip: don't build a giant permanent base early — set up temporary outposts near resource-rich areas. Upgrade your camp via Decoration items to extend the Rested buff duration.

17. How does combat work in Windrose?

Windrose uses Soulslite combat — you must manage Stamina for attacks, blocks, and dodges. Press Ctrl to dodge (has i-frames). Tap block at the precise moment of an enemy strike for a Perfect Block that negates all damage and staggers the enemy. Effective loop: attack 1–2 times → dodge back → wait for enemy approach → heavy/sprint attack. Press T to lock on. You can stack two food buffs simultaneously and respec stats for free at any time.

18. Where do I find iron ore?

Iron ore is found in the Foothills region, reached by progressing the main story. Use a Copper Pickaxe to mine Iron Ore veins. Smelt it into Foothills Iron Ingots for all Lv2 Workbench recipes.

Related: Iron Ore Locations & Uses Guide

19. What's the best early-game food?

Coconuts are the best early-game food. Harvest them from palm trees on beaches — they restore both hunger and thirst at the same time. Stockpile coconuts whenever you see palm trees.

20. How do I unlock more crafting recipes?

Some recipes only appear after you've collected the required materials for the first time. If a recipe isn't showing, try exploring new areas, mining different resources, or progressing the main story. Recipe visibility also depends on your Workbench level.

21. How do I get Sulfur in Windrose?

Sulfur is found in the Foothills region. You need an Iron Pickaxe to mine Sulfur deposits. Sulfur is used to craft Gunpowder at the Millstone (10 Sulfur + 20 Ash = 10 Gunpowder).

22. How do I build a Charcoal Kiln?

Craft a Charcoal Kiln at any open area: 25 Wood + 20 Clay. It converts Wood into Charcoal, which is essential for smelting ores. You need Clay first — mine it from dark soil patches near water.

23. What are Sea Shanties?

Sea Shanties are songs your crew can play while sailing. They provide passive buffs to the crew. You can find new shanties as loot or quest rewards throughout the game.

24. How do I hire NPC crew members?

Visit towns and interact with recruitable NPCs. You need to meet Reputation requirements and pay Piastres. Crew members can man cannons, repair the ship, and fight during boarding actions.

25. Is there PvP in Windrose?

Windrose is primarily a PvE co-op game. There is no forced PvP. All multiplayer is cooperative — up to 10 players working together against AI enemies and bosses.

26. How do I extend the Rested buff?

Build diverse Decoration items in your base. The more unique decoration sub-categories you place, the longer the Rested buff lasts. The Rested buff boosts various stats including stamina regen.

27. What does the Millstone do?

The Millstone is a crafting station that produces Gunpowder (10 Sulfur + 20 Ash) and processes various materials. It requires Iron-tier progression to build.

28. How do I repair my ship?

Carry Wood and Nails on your ship. When damaged, use repair materials from your inventory while near the damaged hull section. Always stock repair mats before long voyages.

29. What are Piastres used for?

Piastres are the in-game currency used to buy ships at the Wharf, hire crew, and purchase items from merchants. Earn them by completing quests, selling loot, and clearing POIs.

30. How do I increase Reputation?

Complete faction quests, clear POIs, and help NPCs. Higher Reputation unlocks better ships, crew options, and merchant inventory. Each faction has its own Reputation track.

31. Can I play Windrose offline?

Yes, Windrose supports single-player offline mode. Your world saves locally. You can also host a world and invite friends for co-op.

32. What's the Ashlands update?

The Ashlands is the first major content update planned for Windrose, at least 6 months after Early Access launch. It will add new biomes, bosses, ships, and story content. The developer confirmed no save/server wipes.
