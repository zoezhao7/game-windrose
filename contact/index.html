Contact Us

Have a question, suggestion, or correction? We'd love to hear from you. Windrose Guides is maintained by community volunteers, and your input helps keep our data accurate and useful for everyone.

Get In Touch

✉️ Email

For general inquiries, content corrections, data contributions, or business matters:

Email: contact@windrose-guides.com

We aim to respond within 48 hours. Please be as specific as possible in your message — include page URLs if reporting an error.

Reasons to Contact Us

🛠️ Report an Error

Found incorrect data? Let us know. Include the page URL and what the correct information should be. We verify all corrections against in-game data before updating.

💡 Suggest Content

Have an idea for a guide we should write, or a topic we haven't covered yet? Tell us what you'd like to see. We prioritize content based on community demand.

📝 Contribute Data

If you've collected verified game data (recipes, stats, resource locations, boss mechanics) and want it added to the site, send it our way with the sources you used to verify it.

💼 Business & Advertising

For advertising inquiries (beyond Google AdSense), partnership opportunities, or other business matters, use the email above with a clear subject line.

📜 Privacy & Data Requests

To exercise your GDPR or CCPA data rights, or for questions about our Privacy Policy, contact us via email. See our Privacy Policy for full details on your rights.

Response Time

We're a small volunteer team. Most inquiries receive a response within 48 hours, though complex data verification requests may take longer. We appreciate your patience and thank you for helping make Windrose Guides better.
