Overview

A stark green draught steeped in wolf fangs. They say that if, after a hard night, you place a dog’s fang under your tongue before sleep, your hands won’t shake come morning. The buccaneers of the archipelago took the idea further and brewed this elixir, hoping to save on bullets. If you missed, it wasn’t the target—it was bait! — Marcel Boucher, buccaneer
