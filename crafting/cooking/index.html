Windrose Cooking Recipes

Food recipes for health regeneration, stamina management, and survival buffs — from coconuts to advanced meals.

Cooking Overview

Cooking is the quiet survival system behind longer Windrose routes. Food helps stabilize hunger and stamina pressure while you gather resources, clear camps, or sail between islands. Unlike potions (which are burst consumables), food provides sustained benefits over time.

The cooking system starts simple — raw coconuts and grilled meat — but scales into multi-ingredient meals with significant stat buffs as you unlock more cooking stations and discover new ingredients.

Early Cooking Route

Follow this order to establish a reliable food supply from the start:

  1. Gather coconuts immediately — They're everywhere near beaches and require no cooking. Eat them raw.
  2. Build a Bonfire — Your first camp. It provides warmth, light, and basic grilling.
  3. Hunt wildlife cautiously — Boars drop Raw Meat but fight back. Only hunt when you can safely recover.
  4. Build a Cooking Fire — Once you have clay. Unlocks better food recipes.
  5. Stock food before expeditions — Always carry 5+ food items before entering caves, sailing, or fighting bosses.
  6. Fish when you get a rod — Fishing Rod (WB Lv2) provides a renewable, safe food source.

Food Strategy Tips

  • Always carry food. Hunger drains your max stamina, which cripples combat. Never explore empty-handed.
  • Coconuts are underrated. They restore both hunger and thirst — perfect for early game and sailing.
  • Save advanced meals for bosses. Don't waste Kitchen recipes on routine exploration. Simple food handles daily needs.
  • Fish is renewable. Once you have a Fishing Rod, you never need to hunt for basic food again.
  • Rum Bottles are dual-purpose. They're used in Combat Repair Kit recipes, so don't drink them all.
  • Stack different food types. In combat, you can eat food + use a potion for combined healing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best early food?

Coconuts — common near palm trees, no cooking required, restore both hunger and thirst.

Do I need cooking for boss fights?

Yes. Prepared meals provide stamina and health buffs critical for long fights where dodging drains your resources fast.

Can food spoil?

Currently in Early Access, cooked food does not spoil. Craft in bulk and stockpile safely.

When should I build a Kitchen?

After you have a Smelting Furnace and Copper Ingots. The Kitchen's advanced recipes need ingredients from Foothills, so there's no rush.

Is fishing worth the effort?

Absolutely. The Simple Fishing Rod (WB Lv2) gives you a renewable, safe food source. Fish can be cooked for solid stamina regen.
