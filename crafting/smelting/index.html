Windrose Smelting Recipes

Convert raw ore into usable metal ingots — the foundation of all advanced crafting.

Smelting Overview

Smelting is the process of refining raw ore into metal ingots. These ingots are essential for crafting tools, weapons, ammunition, and structural components at the Workbench. Before you can smelt anything, you must first build the required stations: the Charcoal Kiln to produce fuel, and the Smelting Furnace to refine the ore.

Prerequisite Stations

You must craft both of these stations before smelting is possible. Build them near each other for efficient workflow.

Smelting Prerequisite Stations
Station Purpose Materials Required
Charcoal Kiln Produces Charcoal (fuel for smelting) 25 Wood + 20 Clay
Smelting Furnace Refines ore into metal ingots 15 Clay + 30 Stone

Smelting Recipes

Place the required materials into the Smelting Furnace along with Charcoal as fuel. Each smelting operation consumes 1 Charcoal.

Output Input Materials Fuel Required
Copper Ingot ×1 6 Copper Ore 1 Charcoal
Foothills Iron Ingot ×1 X Iron Ore 1 Charcoal

Tips & Strategy

  • Gather Clay early. You will need a total of 35 Clay just to build both the Charcoal Kiln and Smelting Furnace. Clay is also used for Clay Pots at the Workbench.
  • Stockpile Wood. The Charcoal Kiln consumes 25 Wood just to build, and you will need additional Wood to fuel charcoal production.
  • Copper is the gateway metal. Prioritize Copper Ore mining and smelting first — Copper Ingots unlock better tools, the Fast Travel Bell, and the Empty Lamp.
  • Iron unlocks Lv2 Workbench. Foothills Iron Ingots are required for the Anvil (30 ingots!) and all iron-tier equipment. Start stockpiling early.
