Windrose Workbench Recipes: Lv1–Lv3

The Workbench is the core crafting station in Windrose. It has three upgrade tiers, each unlocking progressively stronger tools, weapons, armor, and utility items.

Workbench Overview

The Workbench is your first and most important crafting station. Starting at Level 1, it gives you access to basic stone and copper tools. Upgrading to Level 2 unlocks iron-tier equipment and advanced utility items like the Anvil and Sailor Backpack. Level 3 introduces endgame recipes such as the Bosun Backpack and Timber refinement.

Each level requires specific materials to upgrade. Plan your resource gathering around these milestones to progress efficiently through the crafting tree.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I upgrade the Workbench to Lv2?
Gather the required upgrade materials and interact with your existing Workbench. The upgrade option appears once you have the prerequisite items in your inventory. You typically need a significant amount of Foothills Iron Ingots and other mid-game resources.
How do I unlock hidden or special recipes?
Most recipes unlock automatically when you upgrade the Workbench to the appropriate tier. Some recipes also require you to have discovered or crafted prerequisite components first. For example, the Sailor Backpack requires a Torn Sailcloth Bag in your inventory before it becomes visible, and Timber only appears after you have crafted Wooden Planks and reached Lv3.
What is the difference between Repair Kit and Combat Repair Kit?
The basic Repair Kit (10 Wood) restores durability on common tools and structures. The Combat Repair Kit (3 Wooden Plank + 1 Rum Bottle + 1 Steel Nails) is required for repairing weapons and combat gear. The Master Combat Repair Kit is used for high-tier iron equipment.
Which Workbench level do I need for backpacks?
The Torn Sailcloth Bag is available at Lv1. The Sailor Backpack requires Lv2. The Bosun Backpack requires Lv3 and consumes a Sailor Backpack as an ingredient — so plan your upgrades carefully.
