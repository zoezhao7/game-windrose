Crafting Stations

Windrose features several crafting stations, each with its own recipe tree. The Workbench is your primary station, upgraded through three tiers as you progress. Smelting converts raw ore into usable ingots, while Alchemy, Cooking, and Building each serve specialized roles in your survival journey.
