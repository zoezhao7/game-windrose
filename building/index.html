Core Building Philosophy

Windrose's building system is extremely flexible — you can build almost anywhere, both on land and at sea. The key early-game strategy is not to invest heavily in a single permanent base.

Early Game: Temporary Outposts

  • Follow the resources — build a temporary camp wherever you find Copper, Clay, or Iron
  • Minimum camp: Workbench + Cooking Fire + Tent + Chest
  • Move when the local resources are depleted

Mid Game: Permanent Base Criteria

  • Central location with access to multiple biomes
  • Near water for ship docking and Clay access
  • Flat terrain for efficient layout
  • Close to at least one Copper Deposit Mine

Camp Upgrades & Rested Buff

Improve your campsite by building items from the Decoration category:

  • Each unique decoration type extends the Rested buff duration
  • Rested buff provides passive bonuses to crafting speed and stamina regen
  • Experiment with different decoration sub-types for maximum buff duration

Ship-Based Building

Your ship can function as a mobile base. Place essential crafting stations on deck:

  • Workbench + Cooking Fire for on-the-go crafting
  • Storage chests for expedition loot
  • Bed for Rested buff during long voyages
