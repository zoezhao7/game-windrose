Windrose Base Defense Guide: Protect Your Camp (2026)

Passive Defenses

Perimeter Walls

  • Wood walls (most basic) — cheap but weak
  • Stone walls — better durability, requires more materials
  • Palisades — wood spike walls that damage attacking enemies

Natural Defenses

  • Build on elevated terrain — enemies climb slower
  • Use water as a natural moat — build on islands or peninsulas
  • Dense tree lines slow approaching enemies

Active Defenses

Trap Placement

  • Place spike traps at wall breaches and chokepoints
  • Layered defense: outer wall → spike pit → inner wall → panic room

Ship-Based Defense

  • Your ship's cannons can defend coastal bases
  • Position ship broadside to the shore for maximum coverage
  • Combine naval firepower with land-based traps

Raid Preparation Checklist

  • 10+ Bandages in quick-access storage
  • Backup melee weapon near each crafting station
  • Clear sightlines from defensive positions
  • Escape route to your ship (if coastal)

FAQ

Do enemies raid my base?

Yes, enemy factions can raid your base. The frequency and difficulty depend on your faction reputation and base location. Building proper defenses is essential for mid-game survival.

What's the best early-game defense?

Start with a simple wood palisade wall and spike traps at your camp entrance. Upgrade to stone walls once you have a reliable Iron supply. The best early defense is a good weapon — enemies can't raid if they're dead.
